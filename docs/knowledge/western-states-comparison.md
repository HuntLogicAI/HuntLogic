Use this pack for multi-state strategy questions, "which western state should I apply in," point-system comparisons, or "I'm new to western hunting, where do I start" questions.

Point system at a glance (the foundation of any multi-state strategy):

| State | System | How it works | Key implication |
|-------|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| WY | Preference (mostly) + 25% special draw | Random + preference hybrid; squared bonus for some species recently | Skipping years hurts; sheep/moose/goat are 20+ year hauls |
| CO | Pure preference | Linear: highest points draws first | Predictable creep; OTC archery elk is the parallel play |
| UT | Squared bonus | (points² + 1) entries; 50% to max-point pool | Premium tags dominated by long-point holders |
| NV | Squared bonus | (points² + points + 1) entries; 10% NR cap binding | Most NR-hostile cap; trophy units are deep lottery |
| AZ | Bonus + loyalty | Random within bonus-weighted pool; loyalty point per consecutive year | Always nonzero shot; apply every year for loyalty compound |
| NM | NONE | Pure random; 6% NR DIY allocation | Same odds every year; outfitter pool dominates NR allocation |
| MT | Bonus for special permits only | Mostly general-season for NR via combo license | The March 1 combo deadline is the binding gate |
| ID | NONE | First-come-first-served on tag sale day | December 1 sale day is the binding constraint |
| OR | Preference + 5% lottery | Linear preference draw + small random "premium hunt" pool | Apply every year for both pools |
| WA | Bonus for special permits | General-season tags for elk/deer don't need points | Multi-season lottery is the flexibility play |
| CA | Preference | Small tag program overall | Niche; Tule elk is the unique value |

OTC / no-draw opportunities (for hunters who want to hunt every year):
- CO: OTC archery elk in many units, some OTC rifle bull, OTC cow elk via leftover/secondary.
- ID: General tags via December 1 sale day (capped per zone for NR).
- WY: General region elk licenses (1-3 points typical) hunt many units.
- UT: General-season any-bull elk (0-3 points typical).
- MT: General-season elk and deer on NR combo (the combo itself is competitive).
- OR / WA: General-season Roosevelt elk, blacktail, eastern mule deer in many units.
- AZ: Javelina, bear, fall turkey are 0-2 point draws.
- CA: Wild pig year-round, A-zone blacktail OTC for many.

Nonresident-friendly vs hostile (for the "where do I have a real shot" question):
- Most NR-friendly: AZ (bonus + lottery pool), NM (pure random — odds reset every year), MT general combo (if you can hit March 1), ID general (if you can hit December 1).
- Mid-tier: CO (20% NR cap on demand units), WY (25% special draw available, points compound), UT (squared system slow but mathematically defined), OR (preference + lottery).
- Most NR-hostile: NV (10% cap + squared math), CA (small program + heavy resident preference), WA (special permit bonus pool small).

Point creep brutality by species (general west-wide ranking):
- Sheep / moose / goat: 15-30+ year hauls in every state with a point system. Multi-decade commitments.
- Trophy bull elk in famous units (WY 100, CO 61, UT Henry/Pauns, AZ Kaibab, NV Areas 6/7/10): 15-25+ year hauls for nonresidents.
- Trophy mule deer in famous units (CO Bears Ears/Unit 61, UT Henry/Pauns, AZ Strip/Kaibab, NV Ruby Mountains, OR Steens): 15-25+ year hauls.
- Mid-tier quality elk / deer: 5-12 years typically.
- General-season tags: most states have them — 0 points needed.

Recommended starting strategies by hunter profile:

"I want to hunt every year, no point-building patience":
1. CO OTC archery elk + cow elk leftover (annual hunting from day one).
2. ID general-season elk + deer (commit to December 1 sale day).
3. MT NR combo (if you can hit March 1; massive general-season country).
4. CA wild pig (year-round, no draw).
5. AZ javelina + bear + turkey (low-point bonus draws).

"I want trophy bull elk in 10 years":
1. Build points in 2-3 states simultaneously: WY (general region first, then build for limited), AZ (compound loyalty points), CO (preference for mid-tier units).
2. Apply in NM every year (pure random — could draw any cycle).
3. While building, hunt general-season annually via CO OTC archery / MT combo / ID general.

"I want trophy mule deer in 10 years":
1. Build points in CO (preference for Bears Ears or Unit 61) and AZ (Strip/Kaibab bonus).
2. Apply in NV for Ruby Mountains (squared, slow but defined).
3. Apply in NM and OR (premium lottery) for parallel random shots.
4. While building, hunt general mule deer in MT or eastern WA.

"I want a sheep tag in my lifetime":
1. Apply in every state every year — this is a multi-decade math problem.
2. Prioritize states with deferred / random elements: NM (random), AZ (bonus weighting).
3. Build squared bonus in UT, NV — even small point counts compound.
4. Buy points in WY annually for the sheep pool.
5. Accept this is a 20-30 year journey, not a 5-year goal.

"I have a $X budget":
- Under $1K total: CO OTC archery elk + AZ low-point bonus + ID general (if available). Annual hunting on a budget.
- $1K-3K: MT NR combo (~$1K) + secondary state buy-a-point cycles. Annual hunting + long game.
- $3K-10K: All of the above + 4-5 state premium draw applications + occasional drop-camp or outfitter for harder units.
- $10K+: Add fund-raising tags, EPLUS landowner tags in NM, fully outfitted hunts in premier units. Compress the multi-decade wait with money.

Common multi-state portfolio (the "build a hunting career" approach):
- 3-state point-building cycle: pick 3 states with complementary point systems (e.g., WY preference + UT squared bonus + CO preference). Apply in all three every year. Diversifies your "when does the draw hit" timing.
- Annual hunting via 1-2 OTC states (CO archery, ID general, MT combo).
- Lottery shot in NM every year (zero cost to opportunity).
- Sheep/moose/goat applications in every state with a quota — these are the long-arc bets.

Guardrails:
- Don't recommend a state without considering the hunter's flexibility constraints (December 1 for ID, March 1 for MT NR combo, February 14 for AZ spring draw).
- Don't conflate point systems — "I have 5 points in CO" means something completely different from "I have 5 bonus points in UT/NV."
- Don't promise specific draw odds — quotas shift annually.
- Always recommend hunters verify current-year fees, deadlines, and unit boundaries on the state agency website.
- Always anchor recommendations to the hunter's actual goals: trophy quality, fill-the-freezer, annual hunting, lifetime opportunity, budget, time.
