Use this pack for Wisconsin whitetail, bear, or turkey questions; CWD-zone navigation; Northern vs farmland strategy.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- WI DNR Hunt portal lists current season structure and DMU map.
- Annual Combined Hunting Regulations (PDF) is the canonical reference.
- WI DNR bear lottery page tracks current point thresholds by zone.

Deer landscape:
- WI splits into two big personalities: Northern Forest (big-woods rifle hunt, lower density, traditional gun-deer-season culture) and Southern Farmland (high-density ag-country bow hunting, Buffalo County trophy reputation).
- Buffalo, Trempealeau, Pepin, and Pierce counties (SW WI along the Mississippi) are legendary for mature bucks — the "Driftless" hills.
- CWD-positive counties have extended seasons, mandatory carcass rules, and feeding/baiting bans.

Tag structure:
- Resident gun deer license + buck tag included; antlerless tags are purchased via DMU-specific bonus tags ($12 R / $20 NR).
- NR gun deer license: ~$160 + bonus antlerless as needed.
- Archery + crossbow tags are separate purchases ($160 NR archery).
- Bonus antlerless tags in farmland DMUs are nearly unlimited; in northern forest DMUs they're tightly capped.

Season structure (verify current year):
- Archery / crossbow: mid-September through early January (statewide, with brief gun-season pauses in some zones).
- Gun deer (9-day season): opens the Saturday before Thanksgiving — Wisconsin's biggest hunting tradition.
- Muzzleloader: 10 days after gun season.
- Holiday antlerless hunt in CWD zones: Christmas through early January.
- Youth gun and disabled hunts: pre-gun-season weekends.

Bear landscape:
- WI has one of the largest black bear populations in the lower 48 (~24,000+).
- 4 bear zones (A, B, C, D), each with point-based lottery.
- Hounds + bait are legal in WI (one of few states allowing both).
- Class A license: hunter who has been drawn for the kill tag. Class B license: assistance/training-only.
- Point creep: Zone A (north, biggest bears) historically takes 8-12+ preference points for NR. Zones C/D shorter waits.

Bear lottery deadlines:
- Application: early December for the following year.
- Draw results: spring.

Turkey:
- Spring turkey lottery (March deadline) — 6 time periods × multiple zones. Most applicants draw 1st-2nd choice.
- Fall turkey OTC in most zones.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- The "Buffalo County" reputation is genuine — but heavily leased private; access is the binding constraint.
- WI gun deer season opening weekend is a cultural event; public land sees enormous pressure.
- The Northern Forest hunts harder than expected: deer densities can be 5-15 per square mile vs 50+ in farmland.
- Bait + hounds bear hunters often partner with local guides for the first 2-3 years to learn baiting cadence.
- CWD-positive counties affect carcass transport rules — verify the current map before quartering and hauling.

Key deadlines:
- Spring turkey lottery: typically early December.
- Bear lottery: typically early December.
- General hunting licenses: rolling sale, available year-round.

How to answer "should I hunt Wisconsin":
1. For trophy whitetail: SW WI Driftless is genuinely world-class IF you have access. Without private land, frame realistic expectations.
2. For volume deer: any farmland DMU gives consistent shot opportunity, easy bonus antlerless tags.
3. For bear: start applying NOW in Zone A — 8-12 year wait is real and the lottery doesn't reset.
4. For turkey: spring lottery is straightforward; fall is casual OTC.
5. For Northern Forest gun deer: it's a deep cultural experience, not a high-success hunt.

Guardrails:
- Don't quote specific DMU bonus tag availability without verifying current year.
- Don't conflate WI's bait + hounds bear program with northern states that limit one or both.
- CWD rules change annually — always recommend checking the current carcass-transport map.
- Buffalo County trophy reputation is private-land based; flag this honestly for hunters without connections.
