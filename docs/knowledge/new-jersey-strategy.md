Use this pack for New Jersey deer, bear, or turkey strategy. NJ is high-density deer + politically-contested bear.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- NJ DEP Fish and Wildlife Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual NJ Hunting and Trapping Digest is the canonical reference.

Why New Jersey matters:
- One of the highest deer densities per square mile in the US (especially suburban Sussex, Warren, Hunterdon counties).
- Multiple antlerless tags + extended seasons in many zones.
- Bear hunting (when politically open) in NW NJ — small but real population.
- Long archery + short shotgun + muzzleloader + 6-day firearm stack.

Tag structure:
- NR hunting license: ~$135.
- Bow + firearm both require additional permits.
- Antlerless permits often available abundantly (suburban-deer-reduction zones).

Season structure:
- Archery: early September through mid-November + late December into January.
- Six-day firearm: early December.
- Muzzleloader: late November / early December.
- Permit shotgun (specific zones): various.
- Bear hunt: October archery + early December firearm (when open).

Bear hunting (verify current status):
- NJ bear hunt has been politically controversial — opened and closed multiple times in recent years.
- When open: small annual harvest (200-400 bears), limited to NW counties.
- Verify current year status before recommending.

Top regions:
- NW NJ (Sussex, Warren, Hunterdon): high-density deer + bear country, mountain habitat.
- Pine Barrens (S NJ): unique habitat, moderate deer density, traditional hunt culture.
- Suburban central NJ: very high deer densities, archery-friendly via Earn-a-Buck variations.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- NJ suburban archery is genuinely the most underrated east-coast deer hunting — 50-100+ deer/sq mi in some zones.
- NW NJ mountain country (Stokes State Forest, High Point) offers traditional rifle-style deer hunting (with shotgun) + bear opportunity.
- Bear hunt status changes — verify currently open status before recommending.
- Pine Barrens hunting has its own deep tradition; specific permit shotgun seasons.

Key deadlines:
- Antlerless permits: rolling sale.
- Bear hunt application (when open): typically October.

How to answer "should I hunt New Jersey":
1. For nearby residents: yes — accessible suburban deer hunting, possibly bear in NW.
2. For destination hunters: probably no, except as a meat hunt to fill freezer.
3. Verify current bear hunt status before recommending.

Guardrails:
- Verify current bear hunt eligibility — political status changes.
- No rifle hunting — shotgun + muzzleloader + bow only.
- Bow privileges + permit shotgun zones have specific rules; verify current map.
