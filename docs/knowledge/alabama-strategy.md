Use this pack for Alabama deer, turkey, or hog strategy. AL has one of the LONGEST deer seasons in the country and a deep turkey tradition.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- AL DCNR Outdoor Alabama Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual AL Hunting and Fishing Digest is the canonical reference.

Why Alabama matters:
- Among the longest deer seasons in the US — archery + gun runs October through end of January / early February.
- Generous bag limits — typically 1 buck per day, 3 per season + abundant antlerless.
- Strong turkey tradition — historically one of the top US turkey states.
- Year-round wild hog hunting.
- Black Belt (central AL) and river bottoms (Tombigbee, Alabama Rivers) are productive deer country.

Tag structure:
- NR hunting license: ~$320 (season-long all-game).
- 7-day NR license: ~$110.
- Big game license bundled in season license.
- Spring turkey: covered in season license.

Season structure:
- Archery: mid-October through end of January.
- Gun deer: late November through end of January (some zones).
- Spring turkey: March-May.
- Hog: year-round on private + many WMAs.
- Bear: limited season in qualifying counties.

Top regions:
- Black Belt (central AL): prairie + farmland deer country.
- N AL hill country: Bankhead National Forest, public-land deer.
- River bottoms: Tombigbee + Alabama Rivers, high-density deer.
- Conecuh NF (S AL): public hunting access.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- AL season length supports multi-week residence hunters — 100+ day deer season possible.
- Spring turkey in S AL (Eastern subspecies) is legendary — gobblers respond aggressively in early March.
- Bankhead + Conecuh NFs are the public-land backbone.
- Hog hunting is generous; AL doesn't restrict bag limits much.

Key deadlines:
- Licenses: rolling sale.

How to answer "should I hunt Alabama":
1. For long-season deer hunting: yes — one of the longest seasons in the country.
2. For turkey: top-tier southern turkey destination, March opening.
3. For hog: year-round, productive.
4. Bear is limited — niche only.

Guardrails:
- Verify WMA-specific rules — Alabama has many WMAs with unique regulations.
- Spring turkey opening date varies by zone.
- Bear season is small; verify current eligible counties.
