Use this pack for Arkansas deer, bear, elk, turkey, or hog strategy. AR has good bear + a real elk lottery.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- AR Game and Fish Commission Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual AR Hunting Guidebook is the canonical reference.

Why Arkansas matters:
- Real elk lottery — managed herd in Boxley Valley + surrounding Buffalo River corridor.
- Strong black bear hunting in Ouachita + Ozark mountains.
- Long deer seasons.
- Wild hog hunting year-round on private + many WMAs.
- Strong WMA system + Ouachita NF + Ozark-St. Francis NF.

Tag structure:
- NR hunting license: ~$350 (all-game).
- 7-day NR license: ~$60.
- NR archery permit: separate ~$60.
- NR elk lottery application: ~$10 + tag ~$610 if drawn.
- NR bear permit: separate ~$25.

Season structure:
- Archery: late September through end of February (one of the longest in the country).
- Muzzleloader: October-December.
- Modern gun: mid-November through early December.
- Spring turkey: April.
- Bear: archery + modern gun in qualifying zones (October-December).
- Elk (when drawn): October-November.
- Hog: year-round private + many WMAs.

Elk:
- AR has ~500-700 elk in Boxley Valley + Buffalo NR corridor.
- ~30-50 tags annually via lottery (split between R and NR).
- NR multi-year wait; preference point system.
- Lottery is a respected eastern elk opportunity.

Bear:
- AR Ouachita + Ozark bear population is solid (~5,000+).
- Bait + hounds legal in many zones.
- Bear permit + zone-specific tag.

Top regions:
- Ozarks (NW AR): mountain deer + bear country.
- Ouachitas (W AR): bear country + national forest.
- Buffalo River corridor: elk lottery zone.
- Delta + S AR: long-season deer + hog.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- AR's elk lottery is genuinely viable for NR with patience — multi-year wait but draws happen.
- Ouachita NF bear hunting in October over bait is a good east-of-Mississippi DIY opportunity.
- Long archery season + abundant antlerless = high meat-yield potential.
- Boxley Valley is wildlife-viewing rich; some local political tension about elk hunting.

Key deadlines:
- Elk lottery: typically June.
- General licenses: rolling sale.

How to answer "should I hunt Arkansas":
1. For elk: apply for the lottery — best eastern elk opportunity outside KY/PA.
2. For bear: yes — mountain Ouachitas/Ozarks is real, viable DIY.
3. For deer: long season, viable but not destination-tier.
4. For hog: year-round opportunity.

Guardrails:
- Verify elk lottery zone allocations — they're tight.
- Bear method legality varies by zone (hounds, bait).
- Hog hunting WMA rules vary; verify before recommending.
