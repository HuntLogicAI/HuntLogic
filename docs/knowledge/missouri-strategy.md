Use this pack for Missouri whitetail, bear, or elk strategy. MO is a major trophy whitetail state with full OTC access for NR.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- Missouri Department of Conservation (MDC) Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual Spring/Fall Deer & Turkey Hunting Regulations Summary is the canonical reference.
- MDC public hunting areas page lists Conservation Areas (managed for wildlife).

Why Missouri matters:
- Top-5 whitetail state — N MO (Macon, Linn, Sullivan, Adair, Schuyler) produces consistent 150-180" mature bucks.
- OTC tags for NR — no lottery, no waiting.
- Massive Conservation Area system (~1,000 areas, 950,000+ acres) plus Mark Twain National Forest.
- Long archery season + early November rut hunt window.

Tag structure:
- NR firearm any-deer tag: ~$245.
- NR archery any-deer tag: ~$150.
- NR antlerless tags: ~$25 each.
- Bonus tags in some counties.

Season structure:
- Archery: mid-September through mid-January (with brief firearm pause).
- Firearm: November opener (typically the Saturday before Thanksgiving) — 11-day season.
- Antlerless firearm: late November (separate, often 3-4 days).
- Muzzleloader: late December.
- Youth and disabled portions: before/after main seasons.

Top regions:
- N MO grain belt (Macon, Linn, Sullivan, Adair, Schuyler, Pike, Ralls): the trophy zone, comparable to IA SW.
- SE MO bootheel: lower-density agriculture.
- Ozarks (S MO): timber + glade country, more traditional hill hunting, smaller bucks but big public.

Antler restrictions:
- 65 northern counties have 4-point APR (4+ points on one side to legally harvest a buck).
- Verify current APR county list.

Bear:
- MO has a small but growing bear population in the Ozarks.
- Small annual permit lottery — ~400 permits, low draw odds but no wait.
- Resident-only hunting historically; verify current NR eligibility.

Elk:
- MO has a managed elk herd in Peck Ranch Conservation Area (S MO Ozarks).
- Tiny annual draw (~5-10 bull tags), residents only currently.
- Don't recommend MO for NR elk.

Turkey:
- Spring turkey OTC for R + NR.
- Fall archery turkey + brief fall firearm.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- N MO (especially Macon/Sullivan/Adair) is the price-conscious alternative to IA — similar genetics, OTC access, lower cost.
- The MDC Conservation Area system is the best free public-land system in the lower Midwest.
- Bow hunters who target the second week of November in N MO frequently catch the peak rut window — high-leverage timing.
- Late muzzleloader can be productive in agriculture country after combines have finished and deer return to food sources.

Key deadlines:
- Licenses: rolling sale.
- Bear lottery: typically May deadline.
- Elk: resident-only currently.

How to answer "should I hunt Missouri":
1. Yes for trophy whitetail — N MO is the destination, OTC accessible.
2. Hunt early November for rut; combine with public Conservation Areas + Mark Twain NF for cost-effective access.
3. Don't recommend for bear unless drawn (small lottery).
4. Don't recommend for elk to NR — currently resident-only.
5. Spring turkey is a great supplemental hunt.

Guardrails:
- Verify current APR county list — changes occasionally.
- Bear hunting eligibility for NR can shift annually.
- Conservation Area weekday access is dramatically better than weekend.
- Late firearm antlerless season is separate from main firearm — flag the date clearly.
