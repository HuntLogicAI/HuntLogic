Use this pack for Vermont moose, bear, or deer strategy. VT has small but viable big game programs.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- VT Fish and Wildlife Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual VT Hunting & Trapping Guide is the canonical reference.
- FWW publishes moose lottery results and bear/deer harvest data.

Why Vermont matters:
- Moose lottery — small but legitimate (~150-200 permits in recent years).
- Solid bear hunting, especially Northeast Kingdom and Green Mountains.
- Mountain whitetail hunting with deep tradition.
- Statewide antler restriction (typically 2+ points on one side).

Moose lottery:
- Application: typically May-June.
- ~150-200 permits annually across WMUs.
- Resident heavily favored.
- NR draw odds low (typically <5% for most WMUs).
- Subpermittee allowed.

Bear:
- VT harvests ~600-800 bears annually.
- Bait legal (1 month before regular season).
- Hounds legal in some zones with specific permits.
- Regular firearm bear: September through November varies by WMU.
- Northeast Kingdom (Essex, Orleans, Caledonia counties) is the bear country.

Deer:
- Statewide antler restriction (2+ points on one side, with some county exceptions).
- ~10,000-15,000 annual harvest statewide.
- Mountain hunting in Green Mountains; ag-edge in Champlain Valley.
- Long archery + October rifle + muzzleloader stack.

Tag structure:
- NR hunting license: ~$140.
- NR archery: ~$75 add-on.
- NR muzzleloader: ~$60 add-on.
- NR bear tag: included with big game license + ~$5 fee.
- Moose lottery application: ~$10 + tag if drawn (~$250 R, ~$575 NR).

Season structure:
- Archery: October 1 through mid-November + late December.
- Rifle: mid-November (~16-day season).
- Muzzleloader: late November / early December.
- Youth weekend: late October.
- Bear seasons: varies by WMU.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- Northeast Kingdom (NEK) is the wild-feeling Vermont hunt — mountains, lakes, big country, low pressure.
- VT antler restriction has aged the buck herd noticeably; mature bucks more common than 15 years ago.
- Champlain Valley farm-edge whitetail is the highest-density VT deer hunting.
- Bear baiting season runs concurrent with deer archery in some WMUs — combo opportunity.

Key deadlines:
- Moose lottery: typically May-June.
- General hunting license: rolling sale.

How to answer "should I hunt Vermont":
1. For moose: apply for the lottery, accept long odds.
2. For bear: NEK is the destination, viable NR DIY with bait.
3. For deer: VT mountain whitetail is a traditional hunt with modest success; manage expectations.
4. Combo deer + bear in October is the best VT trip structure.

Guardrails:
- Verify current VT antler restriction by WMU.
- Don't oversell moose draw odds — they're genuinely lottery-grade for NR.
- Bear hounds rules vary by WMU; check before recommending.
