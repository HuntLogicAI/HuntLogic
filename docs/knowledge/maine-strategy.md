Use this pack for Maine moose lottery, bear hunting (over bait/hounds), or NE deer strategy. ME is THE moose state in the lower 48.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- Maine IFW Hunting portal lists current season structure and WMD map.
- Annual Hunting & Trapping Laws + Rules are the canonical reference.
- IFW publishes annual moose lottery results and bear harvest data.

Why Maine matters:
- Biggest moose lottery in the lower 48 — ~4,000 permits annually across WMDs.
- One of the strongest black bear populations east of the Mississippi.
- Bait and hounds both legal for bear (one of few states allowing both).
- Adventurous, low-pressure deer hunting in the North Woods (with low success rates).

Moose lottery — the marquee event:
- Application: typically mid-May to mid-June deadline.
- Lottery uses preference points: 1 point per failed application, no cap.
- ~4,000 permits issued annually across multiple WMDs and season types.
- Resident point requirements typically 0-5 points; NR typically 5-15+ points for most WMDs.
- Season types include: Sept (early), Oct (rifle), Nov (cow), Dec (any), plus archery and youth.
- Top WMDs (NW Maine — the "big woods" districts 1-5): NR point requirement 10-15+.
- Subpermittee allowed (one helper, doesn't need to draw, can shoot if permittee defers).

Tag structure:
- NR moose application fee + tag cost: ~$580 NR application + tag fee if drawn.
- NR big game license: ~$115.
- NR bear permit: ~$74 over the big game license.
- NR deer tag included in big game license.

Bear:
- Maine has the highest annual bear harvest in the eastern US (~3,000+).
- 3 legal methods: bait, hounds, trap. Most NR hunters use bait (the dominant guide approach).
- Bait season: late August through late September.
- Hound season: mid-September through end of October.
- Trapping season: October.
- NR hunters often use ME bear guides — $2,500-4,500 typical for a 6-day bait hunt with kill rate around 70-85%.

Deer:
- ME deer hunting is honest big-woods hunting — low density (5-10/sq mi) in much of the North Woods.
- Southern Maine WMDs have higher density and farmland edge.
- Any-deer permit (ADP) lottery for antlerless tags — most NR get a permit in their preferred WMD with 0-2 years of applying.
- Expanded archery zones in suburban areas have extended seasons + multiple tags.
- The "200lb buck" club (deer dressed weight over 200 lbs) is a meaningful Maine tradition.

Season structure:
- Archery: early October (Southern), mid-October (Northern).
- Expanded archery (suburban zones): September-December with multiple tags.
- Firearm: November (~3 weeks, varies by zone).
- Muzzleloader: late November / early December.
- Youth deer day: October.

Turkey:
- Spring turkey OTC.
- Fall turkey limited.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- Maine bear over bait is the most accessible/highest-success big game hunt for an east-coast NR — 70-85% success rates with reputable guides.
- The North Woods is genuinely remote; many camps are accessible only by logging road and most have no cell service.
- Moose hunting in NW Maine WMDs is a multi-day commitment with high success (70-90%) when drawn.
- ME deer hunting rewards tracking — the Benoit family tracking tradition is real and the snow + big country supports it.
- ME's "200lb buck" club is genuinely respected nationally — these are mature deer in a tough environment.

Key deadlines:
- Moose lottery: typically mid-May application, mid-June deadline, results in June.
- Bear permit: rolling sale through summer.
- Any-deer permit lottery: typically August.
- General licenses: rolling sale.

How to answer "should I hunt Maine":
1. For moose: apply every year starting now. NW Maine WMDs are 10-15+ year hauls for NR; southern/central WMDs draw faster but have smaller bulls.
2. For bear: book a reputable guide for a bait hunt — highest east-coast success rate, defining ME experience.
3. For deer: ME deer hunting is a "go for the experience and the tradition" hunt, not a numbers game. Plan for big woods + low density.
4. Spring turkey is a casual add-on.

Guardrails:
- Verify current moose point requirements by WMD — they shift with quota changes.
- Bear guides in ME book up 12-18 months in advance for prime September dates.
- Don't oversell ME deer success — average rifle-season success is 10-15%.
- Verify current bait + hound rules — they've been politically contested.
- ME requires hunter education for first-time hunters; flag this for new hunters.
