Use this pack for Minnesota deer, bear, or moose strategy. Northern forest vs farmland is the first split.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- MN DNR Hunting portal lists current season structure and DPA map.
- Annual Deer Hunting Regulations (PDF) is the canonical reference.
- DNR moose page tracks current population status (currently closed to hunting).

Deer landscape — three personality zones:
- Zone 1 (Northeast, big woods): low density (5-12/sq mi), Boundary Waters wilderness, traditional hunting culture.
- Zone 2 (Central, transition): mixed forest/agriculture, moderate density, average bucks.
- Zone 3 (Southern, farmland): high density, big mature bucks, southeastern bluffland legendary.

Tag structure — DPA-based:
- Each Deer Permit Area (DPA) is designated each year as: bucks-only / hunter choice / managed / intensive harvest.
- Bucks-only: limited antlerless tags by lottery.
- Hunter choice: take either-sex on regular license.
- Managed / intensive harvest: liberal antlerless via bonus tags.
- The DPA designation drives strategy more than zone choice.
- Resident license: ~$34. NR license: ~$185.
- Bonus antlerless tags: $13 for the appropriate zones.

Season structure (verify current):
- Archery: mid-September through end of December.
- Firearm (3A): early November (9-day for most zones).
- Firearm (3B): mid-November (shorter season for Zone 3 farmland).
- Muzzleloader: late November / early December.
- Youth and disabled hunts: October.

Bear landscape:
- MN has a strong bear population, mostly in north/northeast.
- 12 bear management units; lottery-based quota.
- Some BMUs are no-quota (sold OTC); others lottery.
- Bait legal; hounds NOT legal (unique among bear states).
- Resident license ~$44, NR ~$230. Application fee separate.

Moose landscape:
- Minnesota moose hunting is CURRENTLY CLOSED due to herd decline (since 2013).
- Tribal subsistence hunts continue in some areas; state seasons remain suspended.
- If recommending moose to a hunter, point them to ME/AK/NH/Canadian provinces.

Turkey:
- Spring lottery + leftover sale. Most applicants get a tag in their preferred period.
- Fall turkey limited.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- Southeastern bluffland (Houston, Fillmore, Winona counties) is the trophy whitetail country in MN.
- Northern big woods produces fewer but heavier-bodied bucks; quality measured in body weight as much as antler.
- Bear baiting cadence in MN matters — 3-week pre-bait commitment is typical for productive sites.
- The "no hounds" rule shapes the entire MN bear hunting culture vs neighbor states.

Key deadlines:
- Spring turkey lottery: early February.
- Bear lottery: early May.
- Deer licenses: rolling sale through fall.

How to answer "should I hunt Minnesota":
1. For trophy whitetail: SE bluffland is the destination, expect access challenges.
2. For volume + meat hunting: managed/intensive DPAs in farmland Zone 3 are reliable.
3. For bear: apply Zone 1/2 with bait; expect short or moderate wait depending on unit.
4. For moose: don't recommend MN — currently closed.
5. For turkey: easy spring lottery; don't expect lottery stress.

Guardrails:
- Verify current DPA designations annually — they shift based on herd surveys.
- Don't recommend MN moose hunting — season is closed.
- Hounds-for-bear is NOT allowed in MN — flag this if hunter is from WI/MI.
- Bonus antlerless availability varies dramatically by DPA — don't assume statewide.
