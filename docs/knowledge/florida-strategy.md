Use this pack for Florida deer, turkey, gator, or hog strategy. FL has unique Osceola turkey + year-round hog.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- FL Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual FL Hunting Regulations is the canonical reference.

Why Florida matters:
- Osceola turkey — subspecies endemic to FL peninsula. Only place to hunt them in the wild.
- Year-round wild hog hunting on private land + many WMAs.
- Alligator lottery (~6,000-7,000 tags annually).
- Long deer seasons (varies by zone).
- Massive public hunting infrastructure: WMAs, Ocala NF, Apalachicola NF.

Zone structure — critical to FL planning:
- Zone A (S FL): earliest seasons, generally August-September archery openings.
- Zone B (central): September openings.
- Zone C (N central): October.
- Zone D (W panhandle): latest seasons, similar to AL/GA timing.
- Each zone has different archery, muzzleloading, and gun open dates.

Tag structure:
- NR hunting license: ~$152.
- NR big game license: ~$152 add-on.
- WMA permit: ~$26.
- Turkey permit: ~$125 NR.
- Alligator: ~$272 NR + tag.
- Bear: limited season, separate permit.

Season structure (typical Zone B-D):
- Archery: September-October.
- Muzzleloading: October-November.
- General gun: November-January.
- Spring turkey: March-April.
- Alligator: mid-August through early November.
- Hog: year-round private + many WMAs.
- Bear: October in qualifying zones (when open).

Osceola turkey:
- Endemic FL subspecies — smaller, darker than Eastern turkey, with longer leg spurs.
- Hunters complete the "grand slam" (Eastern, Osceola, Merriam's, Rio Grande) by traveling to FL.
- Most accessible via guided hunts on private south/central FL ranches.
- Public-land Osceola hunting on certain WMAs by lottery permit.

Top regions:
- S FL prairie + cattle country: Osceola turkey, swamp deer, gators.
- Central FL: WMA-heavy, mixed game.
- N FL: Apalachicola + Osceola NFs, longer-season deer.
- Panhandle: similar to AL/GA hunting.

Alligator:
- Statewide alligator harvest program — FL has the most accessible gator hunting in the US.
- Lottery for ~7,000 sport tags annually.
- Public + private waters; some applicants draw 1st cycle.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- Osceola turkey is the destination FL hunt — most NR turkey hunters go for the grand slam.
- Year-round hog hunting + free or low-cost tags = aggressive eradication opportunity.
- Alligator lottery is genuinely viable — apply every year.
- Bear hunting in FL has been politically contested — verify current open status.

Key deadlines:
- Alligator lottery: typically May.
- General licenses: rolling sale.
- WMA permits + zone-specific: verify by zone.

How to answer "should I hunt Florida":
1. For Osceola turkey: yes — only place in the world, prioritize a guided spring trip.
2. For alligator: apply for the lottery every year — accessible compared to other gator states.
3. For hog: year-round, productive in S FL prairie + central FL WMAs.
4. For deer: viable but secondary to GA/AL for trophy.
5. Combo Osceola + hog in March is a great trip structure.

Guardrails:
- Zone rules matter enormously — never give generic FL deer advice without specifying zone.
- Bear season political status changes; verify current.
- Osceola subspecies is specific — not all FL turkeys are Osceola (panhandle birds are Eastern).
- Heat + humidity in FL deer season is no joke; plan for early/late season heat.
