Use this pack for South Carolina deer, hog, turkey, or bear strategy. SC has one of the most generous deer programs in the country.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- SCDNR Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual SC Rules & Regulations is the canonical reference.

Why South Carolina matters:
- One of the longest deer seasons in the US — over 4 months on private land in some Game Zones (mid-August through January).
- Generous bag limits — up to 5 antlerless tags + multiple buck tags depending on zone.
- Year-round wild hog hunting.
- Bear hunting in mountain zone (NW SC) — small but real.
- Massive WMA + Francis Marion + Sumter National Forest public access.

Tag structure:
- NR hunting license: ~$200.
- NR big game permit: ~$100 add-on.
- Antlerless deer tag set (date-restricted on WMAs): included.
- Bear tag: separate.

Season structure (varies by Game Zone):
- Archery: opens mid-August in some zones (one of the earliest in the country).
- Primitive weapons: September-October.
- Gun: opens September-October, runs through January in Lowcountry.
- Spring turkey: April-May.
- Bear (NW mountain zone): October-November.
- Hog: year-round on private + many WMAs.

Top regions:
- Lowcountry (coastal SC): longest seasons, highest deer densities, Francis Marion NF access.
- Midlands (central): moderate density, Sumter NF.
- Upstate (NW): mountain bear country, smaller deer.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- SC's August archery opener is the earliest in the country — hunters from cooler states often go for the experience.
- Lowcountry private-land deer with 5+ tag bag limits = serious meat hunting.
- Francis Marion NF gives free public-land access in prime Lowcountry deer country.
- Mountain bear is a small program; consider VA/NC for bigger bear hunts.
- Hog hunting is year-round and aggressive — eradication-mode policy.

Key deadlines:
- General licenses: rolling sale.
- Bear permits: separate, typically September.

How to answer "should I hunt South Carolina":
1. For meat hunters: yes — high bag limits, long seasons, real opportunity.
2. For early-season warm-up: SC August archery is unique.
3. For hog: year-round private + WMA access.
4. For bear: viable but small program; better options in NC/VA.

Guardrails:
- Game Zone rules vary significantly; verify the specific zone before recommending.
- WMA antlerless restrictions are specific (date-limited); flag them.
- August deer hunting is HOT and humid; warn appropriately.
- Bag limits depend on Game Zone + land type (private vs WMA).
