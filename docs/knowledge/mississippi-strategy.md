Use this pack for Mississippi deer, turkey, or hog strategy. MS is a high-quality deer state with long seasons.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- MS Dept of Wildlife, Fisheries, and Parks (MDWFP) Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual MS Outdoor Digest is the canonical reference.

Why Mississippi matters:
- The Mississippi Delta produces some of the largest whitetails in the southern US — comparable to KS/MO in mature buck quality in specific counties.
- Long deer seasons (October through end of January typically).
- Strong turkey tradition.
- Generous bag limits.
- Year-round wild hog hunting on private land + many WMAs.
- Strong National Forest presence (Bienville, Delta, Holly Springs, Homochitto NFs).

Tag structure:
- NR season hunting license: ~$315.
- 7-day NR license: ~$135.
- All-game bundled in season license.
- Spring turkey: covered.

Season structure:
- Archery: early October through mid-November + late January.
- Gun (with dogs / still): November through mid-December.
- Gun (without dogs): mid-December through end of January.
- Primitive weapons: late November / early December.
- Spring turkey: March-May.
- Hog: year-round private + many WMAs.

Top regions:
- Mississippi Delta (NW MS — Bolivar, Sunflower, Washington counties): trophy whitetail country.
- E MS hill country: river-bottom hunting along Tombigbee.
- S MS pine country: Homochitto NF + Bienville NF.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- MS Delta whitetail is the regional secret — high-quality genetics, ag-edge habitat, river-bottom rut hunting.
- Delta NF is the rare public hardwood bottomland deer hunting opportunity in MS.
- Hog hunting is aggressive eradication — generous bag limits.
- Spring turkey in MS hardwood country (E and S) is high-quality.

Key deadlines:
- Licenses: rolling sale.

How to answer "should I hunt Mississippi":
1. For trophy whitetail: yes if you can access Delta private — quality is real.
2. For long-season meat hunting: yes, statewide.
3. For turkey: top southern destination.
4. For hog: year-round opportunity.

Guardrails:
- Delta trophy hunting is heavily leased; access matters.
- WMA-specific rules vary; verify before recommending.
- Bear in MS is endangered/protected; no general hunting.
