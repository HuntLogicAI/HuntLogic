Use this pack for North Dakota deer, mule deer, pronghorn, elk, or sheep strategy.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- ND Game and Fish Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual ND Big Game Proclamation is the canonical reference.
- GNF publishes annual draw statistics by unit.

Why North Dakota matters:
- Mule deer hunting in W ND badlands (Slope, Bowman, Billings, Golden Valley counties) is genuinely top-tier — comparable to W KS / NE Pine Ridge.
- Whitetail hunting throughout state with strong genetics in central/east farmland.
- Pronghorn lottery in W ND.
- Limited bighorn sheep + elk + moose lotteries (long waits).
- World-class pheasant + waterfowl (separate pack territory).

Tag structure — lottery-driven:
- Most ND big game tags are lottery for NR. NR cap typically 1% of total quota for some species.
- Resident gratis tag (free private-land tag) reduces tag availability for NR.
- NR deer license: ~$256.
- NR pronghorn: ~$280.
- NR elk / sheep / moose: separate lottery + much higher tag costs.

Season structure:
- Archery: early September through early January.
- Gun deer: 16-day rifle season starting in early November.
- Muzzleloader: late November / early December.
- Pronghorn: late September through early October.
- Spring turkey: April-May.

Top regions:
- W ND badlands (Slope, Bowman, Billings, Golden Valley): mule deer + pronghorn country.
- Little Missouri River corridor: whitetail + mule deer overlap.
- E ND farmland: high-density whitetail.
- Central + N central: pheasant + sharptail combo with deer.

Mule deer:
- W ND mule deer in the badlands is the destination — heavy buck quality.
- Lottery for NR; multi-year wait for top units.
- Hunt in October-November for bucks at peak vigor.

Pronghorn:
- Limited annual quota; W ND only.
- NR lottery with multi-year wait for top units.

Bighorn sheep / elk / moose:
- Tiny annual lotteries.
- NR effectively shut out for most premium tags.
- Don't recommend ND as primary big game lottery state.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- ND badlands mule deer is the affordable alternative to W states with lower point requirements.
- Theodore Roosevelt National Park + surrounding USFS = unique badland public hunting.
- Pheasant hunters frequently combo with deer in central/east ND.
- The NR cap is real — many premium tags genuinely closed to NR.

Key deadlines:
- Deer lottery (NR): typically June.
- Pronghorn lottery: typically June.
- Elk/sheep/moose: separate lotteries.

How to answer "should I hunt North Dakota":
1. For mule deer: yes if you can draw — badlands is real trophy country.
2. For pronghorn: viable lottery; build points patient.
3. For sheep/elk/moose: NR effectively shut out; don't lead with ND.
4. Combo mule deer + pheasant trip is excellent.

Guardrails:
- Verify current NR allocation by species — quotas change.
- Resident gratis tag system affects tag availability for NR.
- Badlands hunting is real geography — plan for breaks, washes, weather.
