Use this pack for Virginia deer, bear, turkey, or elk strategy. VA's deer regs are county-by-county complex.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- VA DWR Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual VA Hunting and Trapping Regulations is the canonical reference.

Why Virginia matters:
- Long deer season (some counties 4+ months between archery, muzzleloader, firearm).
- Strong bear hunting, especially Allegheny + Blue Ridge mountains; hounds + still-hunt + bait varies by county.
- Statewide spring turkey.
- New elk hunting recently introduced in SW VA (Buchanan, Dickenson, Wise counties).
- Mix of Tidewater coastal hunting + Piedmont rolling hills + Mountain hunts.

Tag structure:
- NR hunting license: ~$160.
- NR big game license: ~$100 add-on.
- Bear license: ~$20-50 depending on residency.
- Archery / muzzleloader / late season licenses: add-ons.
- Elk license: separate lottery (when held).

Season structure (high-level — verify by county):
- Archery: early October through early November (early), and after firearm season through early January.
- Muzzleloader: early November (about 2 weeks before firearm).
- Firearm: mid-November (varies by county) through early-to-late January.
- Late antlerless season: January in some counties.
- Spring turkey: April-May.

Bear:
- VA has one of the largest bear populations in the southeast.
- Multiple bear seasons (archery, muzzleloader, firearm, hound).
- Top bear counties: Bath, Highland, Augusta, Rockingham, Madison.
- Hound hunting permitted in many counties; still-hunting in all.

Elk:
- Recently reintroduced in SW VA — first hunting seasons offered as small lotteries.
- Verify current lottery status and unit boundaries.
- Don't recommend VA as primary NR elk destination — too few tags.

Top regions:
- Shenandoah Valley + Blue Ridge: bear + deer combo, mountain hunting.
- Tidewater (E VA): high deer density, coastal flatland.
- Piedmont (central): rolling hills, productive deer.
- SW VA mountains: bear + elk.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- VA's county-by-county deer regs are genuinely confusing — verify the specific county for season dates and weapon eligibility.
- George Washington National Forest + Jefferson National Forest (GWNF/JNF) = massive public hunting access in mountain VA.
- Shenandoah-area bear hunting is the most coveted VA big game opportunity.
- The new elk lottery is small but real — early adopters got tags.
- Statewide deer harvest is robust (~200,000+) — meat-hunting is reliable.

Key deadlines:
- General licenses: rolling sale.
- Elk lottery: typically late spring (when held).
- Bear seasons stack throughout fall.

How to answer "should I hunt Virginia":
1. For bear: yes, especially Shenandoah/Blue Ridge — strong population, multiple methods.
2. For deer: viable but county complexity matters; verify the specific county rules.
3. For elk: apply for the lottery, accept it's a long shot.
4. GWNF/JNF combo trips for deer + bear are excellent value.

Guardrails:
- County-by-county deer rules are real — never give generic VA deer advice without confirming the county.
- Bear hound rules vary by county and license type.
- Elk lottery is new and evolving — verify current cycle.
