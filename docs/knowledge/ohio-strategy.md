Use this pack for Ohio whitetail strategy. Ohio is a quietly elite trophy buck state with full OTC accessibility for NR.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- ODNR Division of Wildlife site lists current season structure.
- Annual Hunting & Trapping Regulations is the canonical reference.
- ODNR public hunting lands page lists AEP ReCreation Land + state forests + wildlife areas.

Why Ohio matters:
- Consistent producer of mature 150-180" whitetails — comparable to IA/IL/KS in genetics.
- OTC tags for NR — no lottery, no draw, no waiting.
- Strong public-land system including AEP ReCreation Land (60,000+ acres of public hunting on reclaimed strip-mine country in SE OH).
- Lower NR fees than IA/IL/KS.

Tag structure:
- NR archery license: ~$181.
- NR firearm license: ~$181.
- NR adult either-sex deer tag: ~$76.
- Antlerless tags: ~$15 each (county-dependent quota).
- Combo licenses available.

Season structure:
- Archery: late September through early February (very long season).
- "Gun week" (firearm): late November (Monday-Sunday after Thanksgiving) — Ohio's biggest tradition.
- Bonus weekend firearm: mid-December.
- Muzzleloader: early January.
- Youth gun: pre-gun-week weekend.

Top regions:
- Coshocton, Tuscarawas, Holmes, Belmont, Muskingum counties (SE / E-central): river-bluff and reclaimed-mine country, mature buck factories.
- Adams County (S-central): Ohio's most famous trophy county.
- Northern flatland (NW OH): high deer densities, fewer mature bucks, easier access.
- AEP ReCreation Land: ~60,000 acres of free public hunting, multi-county footprint in SE OH.

Antler restrictions:
- A few counties have antler point restrictions (typically 4+ points on one side); verify current map.
- Most of OH has no APR — hunter judgment governs.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- AEP ReCreation Land is the single best free public-land hunting opportunity in the Midwest — reclaimed strip mines create excellent edge habitat. Permit (free) required, available online.
- Coshocton County is the closest OH gets to a "household name" trophy county.
- The two-day firearm bonus weekend pushes deer movement in mid-December — strong post-pressure archery opportunity.
- Ohio's mature buck production is genuinely underappreciated — many hunters who try IA/IL eventually rotate to OH for the price-to-quality ratio.

Bear:
- Ohio bear population is small and growing (eastern OH); no general bear hunting season.
- Don't recommend OH for bear.

Turkey:
- Spring season statewide, OTC permits.
- Fall turkey in some counties.

Key deadlines:
- Licenses: rolling sale, no lottery for general deer.
- AEP permit: free, available online anytime.

How to answer "should I hunt Ohio":
1. Yes, especially for hunters who want trophy whitetail without the IA/IL price tag and lottery.
2. SE Ohio (AEP ReCreation Land + Coshocton/Tuscarawas/Holmes corridor) is the public-land sweet spot.
3. Archery during early-November rut is the highest-leverage time window.
4. Gun week is a cultural experience but heavy pressure on public land.

Guardrails:
- Verify current AEP permit + access rules — they update annually.
- Don't recommend OH for bear hunting — no general season.
- Antler restrictions are county-specific; check the current map before recommending a unit.
- AEP ReCreation Land gets significant hunting pressure during gun week — plan accordingly.
