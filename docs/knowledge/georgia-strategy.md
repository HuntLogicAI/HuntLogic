Use this pack for Georgia deer, hog, turkey, gator, or bear strategy. GA is high-volume deer + diverse small game.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- GA DNR Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual GA Hunting & Trapping Regulations is the canonical reference.

Why Georgia matters:
- Highest annual deer harvest of any southern state (~400,000+).
- Generous bag limits — typically 2 bucks + multiple antlerless.
- Year-round wild hog hunting.
- Alligator lottery (~1,000+ tags annually).
- Bear hunting in N GA mountains + Okefenokee Swamp areas.
- Strong WMA system + USFS National Forests.

Tag structure:
- NR season hunting license: ~$220.
- NR big game license: ~$220 add-on (covers deer + bear).
- Alligator tag (lottery): ~$50.
- Hog tag: free or included.

Season structure:
- Archery: early September through mid-October (statewide), then later in seasons.
- Primitive weapons: October.
- Modern firearm: mid-October through mid-January.
- Spring turkey: March-May.
- Bear: October-December varies by zone.
- Alligator: late August through October (when drawn).
- Hog: year-round on private + most WMAs.

Top regions:
- N GA mountains (Chattahoochee NF): mountain bear + deer combo.
- Piedmont (central): high-density deer, accessible WMAs.
- Coastal plain (S GA): largest deer harvest counties, Oconee NF, longer seasons.

Alligator hunting:
- GA lottery for ~1,000 alligator tags annually.
- Unique opportunity, mostly S GA swamps.
- Apply via early-summer lottery.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- GA Piedmont WMAs are productive deer hunts with good public access.
- N GA bear hunting in Chattahoochee NF is real DIY backcountry hunting.
- Hog hunting is aggressive eradication — generous bag limits, often free tags on WMAs.
- Alligator lottery is a unique add-on for adventurous hunters.

Key deadlines:
- General licenses: rolling sale.
- Alligator lottery: typically June.

How to answer "should I hunt Georgia":
1. For deer: high-volume, generous bag limits, viable for any hunter.
2. For bear: N GA mountains, viable DIY.
3. For alligator: apply for the lottery — unique opportunity.
4. For hog: year-round opportunity, many access points.

Guardrails:
- Verify WMA-specific bag limits and season dates.
- Bear methods vary by zone; verify hounds + bait rules.
- Alligator lottery has zone-specific quotas; verify before applying.
