Use this pack for Maryland deer, sika deer, bear, or turkey strategy. MD has a unique sika deer program on the Eastern Shore.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- MD DNR Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual MD Hunting Guide is the canonical reference.

Why Maryland matters:
- Sika deer on the Eastern Shore (Dorchester, Wicomico, Somerset counties) — only US wild population of Asian sika deer.
- Whitetail throughout the state, high density in central + suburban MD.
- Black bear hunting in western MD (Garrett + Allegany counties) — small lottery.
- Long archery + firearm + muzzleloader stack.

Sika deer (the unique offering):
- Wild population of ~10,000-12,000 sika deer in Eastern Shore marshes and pocosin forests.
- Smaller than whitetail (~80-100 lbs), with elk-like bugling during fall rut.
- Same general deer license + sika tag.
- Pocomoke State Forest is the most-recommended public-land sika destination.
- Reputable guides operate in Dorchester County for full-service hunts.

Tag structure:
- NR hunting license: ~$130.
- NR archery: ~$25 add-on.
- NR muzzleloader: ~$25 add-on.
- Bonus antlerless tags available.
- Bear lottery application: ~$15 + tag if drawn.

Season structure:
- Archery: mid-September through end of January.
- Firearm: late November / early December (~2 weeks).
- Muzzleloader: October + December.
- Bear hunt: limited season in western MD, typically October.

Bear:
- ~150-200 permits annually via lottery for western MD.
- Preference point system.
- NR-eligible.
- Garrett County is the heart of MD bear country.

Top regions:
- Eastern Shore: sika + whitetail combo.
- Central MD: high-density suburban deer.
- Western MD (Garrett, Allegany): mountain whitetail + bear.

Elk:
- Wild elk population recently re-established in western MD; no hunting season yet.

Turkey:
- Spring turkey OTC statewide.
- Fall turkey in some zones.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- Sika hunting is the unique MD experience — comparable elsewhere only to introduced populations on Assateague Island.
- Pocomoke State Forest sika hunting is genuinely DIY-accessible with public land.
- Western MD bear is a respected NR opportunity given the manageable lottery.
- Central MD suburban deer is high-density but private-land dominated.

Key deadlines:
- Bear lottery: typically July.
- Antlerless permits: rolling sale.
- General licenses: rolling sale.

How to answer "should I hunt Maryland":
1. For sika: yes, immediately — unique US opportunity, Eastern Shore is the destination.
2. For whitetail: viable but not destination-grade vs PA/OH/IL.
3. For bear: apply for western MD lottery; manageable wait.
4. Combo sika + whitetail in November is the best MD trip.

Guardrails:
- Sika is a different species — flag this for hunters who assume "whitetail."
- Bear lottery sometimes excludes NR; verify current rules.
- Verify Eastern Shore public-land sika access — some areas seasonally restricted.
