Use this pack for West Virginia deer, bear, turkey, or wild boar strategy. WV is mountain hunting + accessible public land.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- WV DNR Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual WV Hunting and Trapping Regulations is the canonical reference.

Why West Virginia matters:
- Strong bear population — top-5 east-coast bear hunting.
- Massive public land (Monongahela National Forest = 920,000 acres; multiple WMAs).
- NR-friendly deer hunting with reasonable license fees.
- Unique wild boar hunting in the southern coalfields (Boone, Logan, Wyoming counties).
- Long archery + buck firearm + antlerless + muzzleloader stack.

Tag structure:
- NR hunting license: ~$135.
- NR big game stamp: ~$48 add-on.
- Bear license + stamp: separate.
- Wild boar license: ~$15.
- Multiple antlerless tags by county allocation.

Season structure:
- Archery: late September through end of December.
- Buck firearm: late November (~2 weeks).
- Antlerless firearm: separate week in some counties.
- Muzzleloader: mid-December.
- Bear: archery + firearm seasons; hounds + still-hunt.
- Spring turkey: April-May.
- Wild boar: October-January in qualifying counties.

Bear:
- WV harvests 2,500-3,500 bears annually.
- Hounds legal in most zones (with restrictions); still-hunt in all.
- Top bear counties: Tucker, Pendleton, Randolph, Pocahontas, Webster.
- Multiple seasons stack: archery, muzzleloader, firearm, hound permit.

Wild boar:
- Limited wild boar population in S WV coalfields (Boone, Logan, Wyoming, Raleigh counties).
- Different from invasive feral hogs — these are introduced Russian boar populations.
- Limited annual harvest; tag required.

Top regions:
- Eastern Panhandle (Berkeley, Jefferson): closer to DC corridor, mix of farmland whitetail.
- Mountain region (Tucker, Randolph, Pocahontas): bear country, MNF access.
- Southern coalfields: wild boar + bear + deer.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- WV mountain bear hunting is the most underrated east-coast bear opportunity — strong population, big timber, real backcountry feel.
- MNF (Monongahela NF) is the best free public-land big game hunting in the east-coast mountains.
- Wild boar in S WV is a unique niche opportunity — small but real.
- WV's archery seasons run long and allow multiple antlerless harvests.

Key deadlines:
- General licenses: rolling sale.
- Hound permits for bear: typically registration-based.

How to answer "should I hunt West Virginia":
1. For bear: yes — mountain WV is the destination, MNF for free access.
2. For deer: viable mountain or eastern panhandle hunt; not destination-grade for trophy.
3. For wild boar: niche but real, S coalfields.
4. Combo deer + bear + small game in MNF is excellent value.

Guardrails:
- Bear hound rules vary; verify by county and method.
- Wild boar tags + zones are specific; verify before recommending.
- MNF access is enormous but requires planning (logging roads, weather).
