Use this pack for Louisiana deer, turkey, hog, or gator strategy. LA is coastal hunting + unique gator opportunity.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- LA Dept of Wildlife and Fisheries Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual LA Hunting Regulations is the canonical reference.

Why Louisiana matters:
- High-quality deer hunting in Atchafalaya basin, Tensas River area, Sherburne WMA.
- Unique alligator hunting tradition (commercial + sport tags).
- Year-round wild hog hunting.
- Coastal waterfowl emphasis (separate pack territory).
- Strong WMA system + Kisatchie National Forest.

Tag structure:
- NR season license: ~$155.
- NR big game license: ~$155 add-on.
- Alligator: tag-based, allocation system varies.
- Hog: included in big game license on private; specific WMA rules.

Season structure:
- Archery: early October through end of January (zone-dependent).
- Primitive firearms: October-November.
- Gun (with dogs / still): November-January.
- Spring turkey: April.
- Alligator: August-September.
- Hog: year-round private + many WMAs.

Top regions:
- Atchafalaya Basin: bottomland deer + hog + gator combo.
- Tensas River bottomland (NE LA): trophy deer area.
- Kisatchie NF (central LA): public-land deer + turkey.
- Coastal marshes: waterfowl + gator + hog.

Alligator:
- LA allocates ~30,000 gator tags annually to landowners (commercial harvest).
- Sport hunters compete for limited public-land tags via WMA lottery.
- Tags are typically tied to specific marshes or zones.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- Tensas Parish whitetail is the underrated LA trophy zone — bottomland hardwood + ag edge.
- Sherburne WMA + Atchafalaya basin = unique public-land swamp deer hunting.
- Hog hunting in LA is genuinely effective in marsh + bottomland; airboat / dog hunts.
- Gator hunting requires partnering with a tag-holding landowner or drawing a public-area tag.

Key deadlines:
- General licenses: rolling sale.
- Alligator (public) tags: typically July application.

How to answer "should I hunt Louisiana":
1. For unique swamp deer hunting: yes — Atchafalaya/Tensas are real.
2. For gator: niche but unique; mostly partner-based for NR.
3. For hog: year-round, productive in marsh country.
4. For turkey: viable spring hunt; weather can be unpredictable.

Guardrails:
- Verify WMA-specific rules — LA has many area-specific regs.
- Alligator hunting requires landowner partnership or lottery — flag the complexity.
- Bear (LA black bear) is protected; no hunting.
