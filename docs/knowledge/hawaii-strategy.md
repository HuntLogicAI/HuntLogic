Use this pack for Hawaii big game strategy. HI has unique island-by-island hunting opportunities for introduced ungulates.

Tier 1 official anchors:
- HI DLNR Division of Forestry and Wildlife (DOFAW) Hunting portal lists current season structure.
- Annual HI Hunting Regulations is the canonical reference.

Why Hawaii matters:
- All HI big game is introduced — there are no native big game species.
- Year-round hog hunting on most public lands.
- Axis deer on Lanai, Molokai, Maui (introduced from India; expanding population, considered invasive).
- Mouflon sheep on Hawaii (Big Island) — free-range, mountain hunt.
- Wild goat on Kahoolawe + Hawaii — eradication-mode policy.
- Tropical hunting environment — different gear, different tactics.

Tag structure:
- NR hunting license: ~$95.
- Specific tags by species + island.
- Public Hunting Unit access permits (some require registration).

Season structure:
- Pig: year-round on most public lands.
- Goat: year-round eradication on Kahoolawe + parts of Big Island.
- Axis deer (Molokai, Lanai, Maui): year-round on private; managed seasons on public.
- Mouflon (Big Island): managed seasons, archery-friendly.

Top species + locations:
- Axis deer on Lanai: most coveted, mature stags 200+ lbs. Mostly private commercial hunting ($1,500-$3,500 for guided 2-3 day hunt).
- Axis deer on Molokai: similar, often more accessible private ranches.
- Axis deer on Maui: invasive, expanding; some public-land hunting permitted.
- Mouflon on Big Island: free-range mountain hunt, archery + rifle, multiple public hunting units.
- Wild pig on all islands: year-round, archery + firearm + dogs (zone-dependent).
- Goat on Kahoolawe (military access only) + Big Island: eradication-mode.

Public vs private:
- Most "trophy" axis deer hunting is private commercial.
- Public hunting units offer real opportunities for pig + goat + some mouflon.
- HI public hunting requires specific permits and unit registration.

Tier 2 hunter-consensus nuance:
- Lanai axis deer is the iconic HI hunt — small island, fly-in via Lanai City, fully outfitted.
- Big Island mouflon archery is a mountain hunt comparable to western desert sheep hunts in terrain.
- Hog hunting in HI is genuinely productive — large hogs, generous bag limits.
- Tropical conditions, ungulate-dense environment, year-round green forage = unique habitat.

Key deadlines:
- General licenses: rolling sale.
- Public hunting unit registration: rolling, some seasonal.

How to answer "should I hunt Hawaii":
1. For axis deer: yes, especially Lanai — book a guided hunt 6-12 months ahead.
2. For mouflon: viable DIY on Big Island public lands.
3. For hog + goat: year-round opportunity on multiple islands.
4. Combine with a vacation — HI is a destination state in every sense.

Guardrails:
- All HI big game is introduced; flag this for hunters who care about that distinction.
- Lanai access requires booking through specific commercial operations.
- Kahoolawe access is controlled (military lease); not casual access.
- Verify current public hunting unit + tag rules — DOFAW updates seasonally.
